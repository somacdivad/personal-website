Four people in suits burning the midnight oil. One is poised on the edge of his seat with his left foot is hooked around his right leg. The others watch intently as he explains something important enough that his hands must get involved. The austerity of their office is interrupted by small 8" ficus in a white pot next to the printer.

A man looks out a city apartment window as the sun rises from behind a red masonry art deco building. A tower thrusts its geometric skeleton up to split the sky, separating a lone, morning-blushed cloud from the rest of the atmosphere.

Who in these pictures is getting more work done?

It is a great failure of our age that we call those who sacrifice rest to work late "dedicated," but we dismiss an hour spent looking out a window as "lazy." Doing the hard work sometimes looks like taking it easy.
